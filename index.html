Do you handle both AC and electrical on the same visit?
Yes — that's the point of Power Bros TX. HVAC and electric are one team, so a problem that crosses both trades gets solved in one dispatch instead of two companies blaming each other.Sí — HVAC y electricidad son un solo equipo, en una sola visita.
How does pricing work?

Do you work with property managers and apartment communities?
Yes. Multifamily and property-management work is a core part of what we do — HVAC and electrical repairs and installs for units and common areas, all under one number: (408) 912-3778.
Which areas do you serve?

My AC is running but not cooling. What should I check first?
Check the thermostat setting and the air filter — a clogged filter chokes airflow. If the outdoor unit isn't running, check your breaker. Still warm? Call (408) 912-3778 and we'll diagnose it properly — low refrigerant, a leak, or an electrical fault all look the same from the couch.
